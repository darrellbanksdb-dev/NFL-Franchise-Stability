NFL Franchise Stability Analysis

Head Coach and Quarterback Tenure vs Team Success (2016–2025)

Project Overview

Team stability is often discussed as a key factor in professional sports success. In the NFL, frequent coaching changes and quarterback turnover may disrupt team performance, while longer leadership continuity could improve results.

This project analyzes whether head coach tenure and starting quarterback tenure correlate with team wins across NFL franchises from 2016 through 2025.

The goal is to determine whether franchise stability contributes to improved on-field performance.

Business Question

Do NFL teams with longer head coach tenure and starting quarterback tenure achieve more wins than teams with frequent leadership changes?
